DataTalk AI

Natural Language Business Data Analytics - Proof of Concept (POC)

Project Overview

DataTalk AI is a revolutionary platform that allows business users to ask questions about their data in natural language. Instead of writing complex SQL queries or struggling with traditional analytics tools, users can simply type or speak their questions, and our AI system automatically generates and executes the necessary queries.

Target Users: Business managers, finance teams, and decision-makers who need quick insights from their operational databases.
